<objective>
Make `Articulation.Legato` audibly meaningful on the SFZ sampler path. Today every SFZ note reads its source sample from frame 0, so each note retriggers the full recorded bow-start/tongue attack transient at full level — connected string lines "sound like a keyboard is playing them" (composer feedback on the Swan Lake render). Real SFZ players emulate legato without dedicated legato samples by (a) offsetting the sample start past the attack transient and (b) applying a longer envelope attack to mask the seam. This task implements both, gated to `Articulation.Legato` only.

Purpose: musically-correct legato phrasing on the shared SFZ surface (VSCO CE strings/winds), fixing the "synthy, no transition from one tone to the next" report — without touching any other articulation's bytes and without new build guards (SFZ is Web-stripped; Desktop-only path).

Output: a Legato-only offset + softened-attack change in `SfzRenderer.cs` (byte-identical for every non-Legato articulation) + a focused numeric xUnit suite proving the offset, the softened attack, non-Legato byte-identity (golden-hash pin), and two-run determinism.
</objective>
