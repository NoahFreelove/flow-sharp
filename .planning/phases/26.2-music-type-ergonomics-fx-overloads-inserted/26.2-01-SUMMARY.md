## Decisions Made

1. **Skip-attribute strategy** — Used `[Fact(Skip = "...")]` rather than `#if false` for Wave-1+ pending facts. Reason: When a later wave's planner removes the Skip attribute, the fact auto-executes; with `#if false`, the planner would also have to remember to flip a compile flag. Lower-friction for downstream waves.
2. **No `[Collection("FlowScripts")]`** — Mirrored the QuickFixes/DecibelBeatNumericCompatFacts.cs pattern (which works without the Collection serialization). The Phase 26.1 Dict files use it because they print to stdout for assertions; our facts use `GetVariable<AudioBuffer>().Data` and don't share Console.SetOut state.

## Threat Surface Scan
