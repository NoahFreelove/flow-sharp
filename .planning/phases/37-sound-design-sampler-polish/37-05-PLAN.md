**A6 rationale (RESEARCH Pattern 10):**
- A4 puts a sample point at the bottom of the flute's most expressive register where embouchure timbre changes are most audible.
- G4→A4 = 2 semitone varispeed stretch (low magnitude → low timbre artifact).
- G4→D5 = 7 semitone varispeed stretch (higher magnitude — keeping D5 as the gap-closer would leave the LOW register thinner).
- Composer perceives "fuller low register" more readily than "fuller upper register" in ragtime/classical melodies.
- Both options sum to ≤ 200 KB per file (well under CLAUDE.md SPEC D-02's 5 MB total cap); composer can add D5 later if needed.
