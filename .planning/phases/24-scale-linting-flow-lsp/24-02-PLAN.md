Mode interval patterns (W=2 semitones, H=1 semitone), used to derive each mode's spellings from a root:
- Major (Ionian):     W W H W W W H = 0  2  4  5  7  9  11
- Dorian:             W H W W W H W = 0  2  3  5  7  9  10
- Phrygian:           H W W W H W W = 0  1  3  5  7  8  10
- Lydian:             W W W H W W H = 0  2  4  6  7  9  11
- Mixolydian:         W W H W W H W = 0  2  4  5  7  9  10
- Minor (Aeolian):    W H W W H W W = 0  2  3  5  7  8  10
- Locrian:            H W W H W W W = 0  1  3  5  6  8  10

Letter rotation must follow C-D-E-F-G-A-B (modulo 7) starting from the root's letter — this is what makes the spellings "spelling-aware" rather than just chromatic. For example F major's 4th degree is the letter Bb (not A# even though they sound the same), because the letter sequence from F is F-G-A-B-C-D-E and the interval table places the 4th at semitone 5 above F = B-flat (semitone 10), not A-sharp.
