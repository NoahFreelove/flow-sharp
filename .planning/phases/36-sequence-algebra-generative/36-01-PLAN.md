<objective>
Establish the Phase 36 PRNG determinism foundation by shipping `Runtime/PrngRegistry` keyed by `(SourceLocation, generator-name)` per D-v1.5-06 + D-36-09, integrating it into `ExecutionContext` lifecycle (snapshot/restore for hermetic isolation), reseeding at `renderSong` / `writeWav` boundaries to preserve the two-run cmp-clean determinism contract (Phase 18/25/27/28/29/33 inheritance), and creating the bash harness `scripts/test_two_run_determinism.sh` that downstream plans will invoke.

Also establishes a CI source-grep gate that blocks any future `new Random(` construction inside `StandardLibrary/Patterns`, `StandardLibrary/Generative`, `StandardLibrary/Improv` — this gate fires from Plan 36-05 onward as those directories get created.

Purpose: Every PRNG-driven primitive added in later Phase 36 plans (degrade, sparseSeq, sometimes, markov, lsystem, cellular, life, lorenz, logistic, jam) MUST route through `PrngRegistry`. Without this foundation, Phase 36's primitives would individually call `new Random()` from wall-clock and break the inheritance contract.

Output: PrngRegistry singleton; ExecutionContext extension; FlowEngine boundary hooks; TestSnapshot extension; xUnit facts; bash harness.
</objective>
