<objective>
Ship SPEC-4 lexer changes: three new line-comment forms recognized by `SkipWhitespaceAndComments`. Each is position-sensitive (column-0 with optional leading whitespace, gated by `IsStartOfLineContent()`) mirroring the existing `Note:` arm at `SimpleLexer.cs:1144`.

The three new arms:
1. **`;` at column-0** → line comment to end-of-line (D-11 Option A). Mid-line `;` stays `TokenType.Semicolon` — preserves every shipping pragma + statement terminator. RESEARCH grep audit confirms zero column-0 `;` exist in any in-repo `.flow` file.
2. **`TODO:` at column-0** → line comment to end-of-line.
3. **`FIXME:` at column-0** → line comment to end-of-line.

`Note:` is ALREADY shipping per RESEARCH §Summary finding 1 (SimpleLexer.cs:1144 — `IsStartOfLineContent()`-gated). This plan does NOT touch the existing `Note:` arm. The TextMate grammar side (which IS new for `Note:`) ships in Plan 31-06.

Purpose: Make `tutorial.flow`'s chapter-divider `Note:` convention first-class (grammar side), unify with `TODO:`/`FIXME:` lead-ins composers expect, and add Lisp-tradition `;` Logan-style comments without breaking the statement-terminator role.

Output: 1 modified lexer file + 1 new test fact class.
</objective>
