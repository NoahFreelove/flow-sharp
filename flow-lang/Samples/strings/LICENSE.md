- D4.wav — from Violin.arco.ff.sulD.D4
- D5.wav — from Violin.arco.ff.sulG.D5

Mixed-instrument bundle: viola at D3 (violin physically cannot reach D3 — open G is
its lowest string), violin at D4 and D5. Both are bowed (arco) at fortissimo. The
viola provides a richer low-register timbre; the SampledInstrumentRenderer's
varispeed-shift around D3 keeps the viola sound family-consistent with the violin
samples at higher pitches.

Original recordings: University of Iowa Musical Instrument Samples (MIS) project.
Conversion: 24-bit stereo AIFF → 16-bit mono WAV via ffmpeg, 2026-05-11.
